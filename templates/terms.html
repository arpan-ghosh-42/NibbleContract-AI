2. What NibbleContract AI does

The service helps students judge internship opportunities. It has two checks:

  • Check a job post: before you apply, it looks for warning signs and good signs in an internship post.
  • Check an offer letter: after you're selected, it reviews the letter's terms and estimates how trustworthy the offer looks.

Each check produces a trust score from 0 to 100, a risk level (Low, Medium or High risk) and the evidence behind it. The service never declares that a company is a scam or that an offer is genuine. It shows signals and sources so you can make your own decision.

3. How results are produced

Results depend on two things: the text you provide, and the public information available on the web at the moment you run a check.

  • AI analysis. Artificial-intelligence models read your text and look for known warning signs and good signs. AI can misread text, miss things or make mistakes.
  • Online research. Automated agents search public sources: web search results, Reddit discussions, news coverage, domain registration records and the company's own website. We don't control these sources, and they can be incomplete, outdated, biased or wrong.
  • Less information means less certainty. New or small companies often have little or no online presence. That is not evidence against them, and the score for such companies rests on less information.
  • Results can change. Running the same check again, or on another day, can give a different result as models vary and online information changes.
  • Fixed scoring rules. The score comes from fixed rules applied to the evidence found. The rules can't capture every situation, and a high score does not mean an opportunity is safe.
