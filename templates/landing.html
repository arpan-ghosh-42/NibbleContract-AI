    Built to be honest

    An AI that shows its work, and admits its limits

    A confident wrong answer is worse than none. So the tool shows a risk level and its evidence, never a verdict on the company.

    • Quotes are verified. If the AI's quoted clause isn't in your document, it's marked, not trusted.
    • The score is rule-based. The AI only finds evidence. It can't pick the number.
    • Your document stays private. Processed in memory, never written to disk. Only the company's domain and name are used for lookups.
    • No data isn't a verdict. Many real small companies have no online footprint, and we say so instead of guessing.
    FAQ

    Questions you might have

    Is this legal advice?

    No. It helps you spot things worth asking about. For anything important, ask your college placement cell or someone you trust before you sign or pay anything.

    Is my offer letter stored?

    No. Uploads are processed in memory and never saved to disk or logged. Your saved reports live only in your own browser and contain the report, not the full letter.

    Can it prove an internship is real?

    No tool can from a document alone. It looks for known warning signs and checks public signals like domain age and reviews, then gives you a checklist to verify the company yourself on its official website.

    What if it finds nothing about my company?

    That's common for small or new firms and is reported as "no public discussion", not as a warning. Use the verification checklist and ask the company for a call with a named employee.

    What do the research agents do?

    They gather evidence and nothing else. The reputation agent searches the web and Reddit, the company agent checks the domain, website and careers page, and the funding agent looks for funding news. Every finding must point to a link a tool really returned in that run, and fixed rules, not the AI, turn the evidence into the score.

    Which languages and formats work?

    English text in PDF or .txt files up to 5 MB. Scanned images aren't readable, so paste the text instead.
